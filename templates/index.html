FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need technical skills?
No. If you can describe your business in a paragraph, you can launch a Gabby agent. The only "code" involved is pasting one snippet onto your website — and every major site builder (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix) has a spot for that.
What happens when the agent doesn't know an answer?
It says so honestly instead of guessing, and offers to take the customer's details so you can follow up. Every conversation lands in your dashboard, so you can add the missing answer once — then it knows it forever.
Who owns my data?
You do. Your business content, your conversations, your customer messages — all yours. Export or delete everything anytime, no hoops, no retention games.
How much does the AI cost me per chat?
Nothing extra. Starter and Growth include unlimited chats at one flat monthly rate — no per-message metering and no surprise bills at the end of the month.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no contracts and no cancellation fees. Your agent stays live until the end of your billing period, and your data remains exportable afterward.
Can Gabby answer phone calls too?
Not yet — chat is where we started, because it's where most businesses lose customers after hours. Voice calling is on the roadmap, and beta customers will be first in line when it lands.
