# About TryGabby

TryGabby builds AI chat agents for businesses of every size — from solo shops to large companies. Every business gets the same attentive support. A TryGabby agent lives on your website and answers customer questions 24/7, even when you're closed or busy.

## How it works

Getting started takes about 10 minutes:
1. Tell us about your business — name, website, and a greeting message.
2. TryGabby crawls your website to learn your products, services, and business hours.
3. Paste one snippet of code on your site just before the closing body tag. The chat bubble appears automatically and starts answering visitors.

## Features

- Answers customer questions 24/7 using knowledge from your own website.
- Knows your business hours and tells customers when you're open or closed.
- Captures leads: collects the visitor's name, phone number, and email, plus their preferred contact method (call, text, or email).
- Conversation starters: suggested questions that invite visitors to chat.
- Custom widget color to match your brand.
- Owner dashboard: edit your agent, manage its knowledge, test-chat, and read incoming leads in a leads inbox with unread badges.
